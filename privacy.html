Talos Backup (Google sign-in)

"Talos Backup" is an internal tool. It signs in to the Google account of Talos-Trades LLC so the company can copy its own files to its own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public.

  • It reads and writes files only in the company's own Google Drive, to make and restore backups.
  • Google user data it accesses is not sold, shared with other people or companies, or used for advertising.
  • Google user data is not used to train AI or machine learning models.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept on company computers only.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
